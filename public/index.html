        How the deal score works

        Each truck is scored out of 100 from five parts. Price against market is worth 35 points, and compares the asking price plus any transfer fee with an estimate built from 76 CrewMax 4x4 5.7L asking prices seen in this search, adjusted for year, miles and trim. Miles for its age is worth 20, year and trim 15, history 15 (no accidents, clean title, one owner), and logistics 15 (close local dealers score highest; transfers and long drives score lower).

        Over 70 is a strong deal, 62 to 69 good, 52 to 61 fair, and anything lower is a pass unless something else makes it special. A price far below market with no clean-title record gets capped and flagged.

        What to check on any 2014 to 2021 5.7L

        • Secondary air injection pump and valves. This is the common big-ticket 5.7L failure, more likely in cold winters. Scan for codes and ask if it's been replaced.
        • Water pump seepage around 100k and up. Look for pink crust at the front of the engine.
        • Frame and underbody rust. Utah salts its roads, so look hard at the frame, brake lines and spare tire hanger.
        • Transmission fluid service records past 100k miles.
        • Open recalls. Plug the VIN into nhtsa.gov/recalls before you buy.
        • The 5.7L uses a timing chain, so there's no belt job coming. Flex-fuel (FFV) trucks run fine on regular gas.
